Discover sake through aromas you love. A light, easygoing bottle made together by Urazato Brewery and SAKE ART TOKYO.

About this site

This is the official SAKE ART TOKYO / Chill Labo website. There are no registration forms, analytics SDKs or advertising tags. Only your age-confirmation choice is stored in this browser.

Images and the game

Images and the game are served through GitHub Pages. Opening them sends ordinary connection details, such as your IP address, to the hosting service. The page and the 30-second CPU game exchange start, pause and end events. Game results are not saved or sent outside this page, and existing game records are not changed. Game outcomes say nothing about a sake’s flavor, quality or drinking safety.

Saving label artwork

The save button lets you keep the original label image for the selected sake. On iPhone and iPad, use the share menu or press and hold the image. On a computer, it downloads as a PNG. External shops and Instagram have their own terms and policies.
