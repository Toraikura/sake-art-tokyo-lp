URAZATO / THE BREWERY

Brewing in
Yoshinuma.

Urazato Shuzo-ten is a sake brewery in Yoshinuma, Tsukuba, Ibaraki, founded in 1877.

The brewery lists Kiritsukuba, Fukuwarai and Urazato among its brands. Its Urazato line was launched by sixth-generation brewer Chikara Urazato with a clear theme: pursuing mastery of Ogawa yeast.

BREWING NOTES

Ogawa yeast.
Brewery context.

Urazato Brewery describes Ogawa yeast as a low-acid yeast that ferments well at low temperatures and produces a delicate aroma. The Urazato brand explicitly frames its work around exploring this yeast.

At the same time, we do not fill in unpublished SAT 001 specifications—such as its exact rice, polishing ratio or yeast—from assumptions. Brewery-wide practice and bottle-specific specifications remain separate.
