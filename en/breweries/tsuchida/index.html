TSUCHIDA / THE BREWERY

Brewing with
microbes.

Tsuchida Brewery is a sake brewery in Kawaba Village, Gunma, founded in 1907.

The brewery says it returned all of its sake production to kimoto brewing in 2019, working with rice, rice koji, water and microorganisms without other additives. Its long-established local label is Homare Kokko.

SAT 002 / SENSORY

Banana.
Cacao.

Reminiscent of banana and cacao.
These are sensory words.

They are not analytical claims that a specific molecule has been identified as the cause in SAT 002. In general sake sensory evaluation, isoamyl acetate is used as a reference for banana-like aroma, but that does not make it the confirmed cause of this bottle's sensory profile.

BREWING NOTES

Brewery method.
Bottle specs.

Tsuchida Brewery describes a direction built around all-kimoto brewing, the work of microorganisms, and using local Gunma table rice with minimal polishing where appropriate. It also notes that rice origin and polishing ratio can vary by label and purpose.

For that reason, we do not copy brewery-wide practice into SAT 002 as if it were this bottle's confirmed rice, polishing ratio or analytical data. Bottle-specific facts and brewery context remain distinct.

SENSORY ≠ ANALYSISBanana and cacao are tasting descriptions. They do not mean a causative aroma compound has been identified by analysis.
