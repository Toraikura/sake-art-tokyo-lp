01 / THE BAR

CHILL LABO

At Chill Labo, a sake bar in Akasaka, guests discover different bottles one glass at a time. One question kept coming back.

↓

02 / THE QUESTION

“Can I buy a bottle
to take home?”

A favorite glass did not have to end at the bar. That question became the starting point for retail—and then for making something of our own.

↓

03 / FROM GLASS TO BOTTLE

From a glass
to a bottle.

Rather than simply selling finished sake, we wanted to go deeper into each brewery's character and create a new expression together.

↓

04 / SAKE ART TOKYO

BRAND, NOT BAR.

Chill Labo remains the independent bar experience. SAKE ART TOKYO is the brand that works with breweries on bottles beyond the bar. “From Chill Labo” stays as a line about where the story began, not as a merged entity name.
